Add Ons (cb-add-ons) are packages that add new features to CollectionBuilder projects, providing self-contained dependencies, templates, and recipes. They generally support unusual formats or functionality beyond Items found in typical collections. Since these are unique features, cb-add-ons might require some customization for your content, but provide a base that open new possibilities!
