### Origin

OHD was first built in 2018 at the University of Idaho Library's [Center for Digital Inquiry and Learning (CDIL)](https://cdil.lib.uidaho.edu/). It has since been used for projects ranging from queer history documentation to wilderness research archives to large-scale podcast collections.

Visit the [OHD project site](https://oralhistoryasdata.github.io/) for full documentation and news.

### Features

- Apply your own thematic codes to transcripts — OHD generates automatic color-coded visualizations
- Synchronized audio and video playback with transcript segments (YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud, MP3)
- Timestamp-based navigation — jump to specific interview moments from any visualization
- CSV-driven workflow — manage transcripts in Google Sheets or any spreadsheet tool
- Interactive filtering by theme, speaker, and keyword
- All standard CollectionBuilder features alongside your oral history content

### Example Projects

- [**OHD Demo**](https://oralhistoryasdata.github.io/template/) — demonstration site with sample interviews
- [**Voices of Gay Rodeo**](https://www.voicesofgayrodeo.com/) — documenting LGBTQ+ experiences in western rodeo culture
- [**Taylor Wilderness Research Station Archives**](https://cdil.lib.uidaho.edu/taylor-archive/) — interviews with wilderness researchers, students, and caretakers
- [**CTRL+Shift**](https://ctrl-shift.org/) — examining poets' digital writing practices
