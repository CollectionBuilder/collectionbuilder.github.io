### Origin

CB-Essay grew out of work with graduate students at the University of Idaho Library's [Center for Digital Inquiry and Learning (CDIL)](https://cdil.lib.uidaho.edu/) who were building multimodal essays for their theses. The students needed a way to combine long-form writing with collection items on the web. The existing tools weren't cutting it, so we built CB-Essay.


### Example Projects

- [**CB-Essay Demo**](https://collectionbuilder.github.io/cb-essay/) — self-documenting demo that teaches you the features as you read
- [**Tender Spaces**](https://cdil.lib.uidaho.edu/tender-spaces/) — multimodal essay on Gaëtane Buttigieg (Alicia Gladman)
- [**Sedimentation**](https://cdil.lib.uidaho.edu/sedimentation/) — an archive of Glen Canyon (Hannah Green)
- [**Fire Lines**](https://cdil.lib.uidaho.edu/fire-lines/) — an archive-driven exploration of the Legacy of 1910's Great Fire in the Northwest (Isabel Marlens)
