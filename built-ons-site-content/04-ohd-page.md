### What Is Oral History as Data?

Oral History as Data (OHD) is a framework for publishing and analyzing coded oral history and qualitative interview transcripts on the web. You prepare your transcripts as CSVs with speaker identifications, thematic tags, and timestamps, and OHD transforms them into interactive, color-coded visualizations that reveal patterns across your interviews.

Like CB-Essay, OHD is built on CollectionBuilder, so you also get Browse pages, maps, timelines, and all the other collection features alongside your oral history content.

---

### Origin

OHD was first built in 2018 at the University of Idaho Library's [Center for Digital Inquiry and Learning (CDIL)](https://cdil.lib.uidaho.edu/). It has since been used for projects ranging from queer history documentation to wilderness research archives to large-scale podcast collections.

---

### Features

**Oral history analysis:**
- Apply your own thematic codes to transcripts
- Automatic color-coded visualization of themes across interviews
- Interactive filtering by theme, speaker, and keyword
- CSV-driven workflow — manage transcripts in Google Sheets or any spreadsheet tool

**Media integration:**
- Synchronized audio and video playback with transcript segments
- Support for YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud, and MP3 files
- Timestamp-based navigation — jump to specific interview moments

**Collection features:**
- All standard CollectionBuilder features: Browse, Map, Timeline, Subjects, data downloads
- Metadata-driven item pages for each interview
- Responsive design across all devices
