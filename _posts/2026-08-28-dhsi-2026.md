In June 2026, Devin and I taught a week-long course, “Creating Digital Collections with Minimal Infrastructure: Hands On with CollectionBuilder for Teaching and Exhibits”, at [Digital Humanities Summer Institute](https://dhsi.org/) (DHSI).
Amazingly, this is our sixth time teaching the course (twice on Zoom, twice at University of Victoria, twice at Université de Montréal).
Each iteration and group is a little different and we have really enjoyed the opportunity.

This year's crew of learners was a great mix, from undergrad to senior faculty, different fields, different institutions, different projects and use cases--a joy to collaborate with everyone, get new questions, and explore new ideas.
Everyone was very engaged, doing some amazing trouble shooting, data wrangling, deep thinking, and CollectionBuilder-ing!

Folks created collections exploring perfumes, museum collections, family archives, digital editions, sacred sites, oral histories, and even Pete Seeger.
You can explore projects created during the week in the [DHSI Show and Tell 2026 collection](https://collectionbuilder.github.io/dhsi2026-share/) or checkout the [course outline](https://osf.io/skfhw).
